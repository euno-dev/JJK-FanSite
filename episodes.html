Jujutsu Kaisen 0: The Movie

Jujutsu Kaisen 0 — The Movie

This movie takes place before the main series and focuses on Yuta Okkotsu, a high schooler haunted by the curse of his childhood friend Rika. Gojo brings him to Tokyo Jujutsu High where he learns to control his powers and joins the battle against powerful curses.

Season 1 - Episodes

Episode 1 — Ryomen Sukuna

Yuji Itadori encounters cursed objects and becomes host to Sukuna.

Episode 2 — For Myself

Yuji begins training with Megumi and Nobara under Gojo's guidance.

Episode 3 - Girl of Steel

Team missions reveal the dangerous world of curses and exorcisms.

Episode 4 - Fearsome Womb

Yuji, Megumi, and Nobara are sent to investigate a juvenile detention center that has become a site for a potentially special-grade cursed spirit.

Episode 5 - Fearsome Womb, Part 2

Sukuna takes full control of Yuji's body, rips out his heart, and confronts a fleeing Megumi.

Episode 6 - After the Rain

Yuji Itadori makes a pact with Sukuna to be resurrected after his death, while Megumi Fushiguro and Nobara Kugisaki train for the upcoming Goodwill Event.

Episode 7 - Assault

The powerful special grade cursed spirit Jogo ambushes Satoru Gojo, but Gojo effortlessly demonstrates his immense power by using Domain Expansion and his innate technique, "Infinity"

Episode 8 - Boring

Focuses on the first-year students' interactions with students from Kyoto Jujutsu High, specifically Aoi Todo and Mai Zen'in, during a lull in the training event.

Episode 9 - Young Fish and Reverse Punishment

Primarily introduces the character of Junpei Yoshino and his tragic encounter with the curse Mahito, while Yuji Itadori begins a new investigation with a first-grade sorcerer.

Episode 10 - Idle Transfiguration

Focuses on the parallel investigations of Yuji Itadori and Kento Nanami, which lead them both to the special-grade curse, Mahito.

Episode 11 - Narrow-Minded

Concludes the fight between Kento Nanami and Mahito while focusing on the tragic manipulation of Junpei Yoshino by Mahito, which ultimately pushes Junpei onto a dark path.

Episode 12 - To You Someday

A heartbreaking episode that details the tragic end of Junpei Yoshino and the beginning of Yuji Itadori's direct and enraged confrontation with the special-grade curse Mahito.

Episode 13 - Tomorrow

Concludes the intense battle against the special-grade curse Mahito, ending the "Small Fry and Reverse Retribution Arc" with a mix of action, tragedy, and reflection. concludes the intense battle against the special-grade curse Mahito, ending the "Small Fry and Reverse Retribution Arc" with a mix of action, tragedy, and reflection.

Episode 14 - Kyoto Sister School Goodwill Event - Team Battle, Part 0

Marks the beginning of a new arc, transitioning from the dark tone of the previous episodes to the introduction of new characters and a planned competition.

Episode 15 - Kyoto Sister School Goodwill Event - Team Battle, Part 1

Kicks off the first day of the goodwill event with an immediate, all-out ambush on Yuji Itadori, leading to a surprising, action-packed confrontation with Aoi Todo.

Episode 16 - Kyoto Sister School Goodwill Event - Team Battle, Part 2

Focuses heavily on the separate, intense character battles between the students, particularly an unexpected showdown that reveals the true nature of Panda.

Episode 17 - Kyoto Sister School Goodwill Event - Team Battle, Part 3

Dives into the personal conflicts and backstories of several female sorcerers, particularly the strained relationship between the twin sisters Maki and Mai Zenin.

Episode 18 - Sage

Concludes the battle between Megumi and Kamo and features a shocking twist as the goodwill event is interrupted by an invasion of special-grade cursed spirits.

Episode 19 - Black Flash

Amps up the fight against the special-grade curse Hanami, as Yuji Itadori, Aoi Todo, and the other students are forced into a desperate battle for survival.

Episode 20 - Nonstandard

Intensifies the battle between the sorcerers and the special-grade curse Hanami, showcasing the successful coordination of Yuji Itadori and Aoi Todo and the subsequent intervention of Satoru Gojo.

Episode 21 - Jujutsu Koshien

Serves as a transitional episode. It wraps up the events of the curse invasion and shifts the tone with a lighthearted but competitive baseball game to conclude the sister school exchange event.

Episode 22 - The Origin of Obedience

Shifts focus away from the main cast in Tokyo to introduce the Cursed Womb: Death Paintings and details a tragic past mission involving the second-year sorcerer, Maki Zenin.

Episode 23 - The Origin of Obedience, Part 2

Concludes the Cursed Womb: Death Painting arc with an explosive, high-stakes battle as the first-year sorcerers face off against the formidable special-grade cursed brothers, Eso and Kechizu.

Episode 24 - Accomplices

Serves as the season finale. It provides closure to previous events and sets up future storylines while showcasing the strength of the first-year team and introducing a new antagonist.

Season 2 - Episodes

Episode 1 — Hidden Inventory

Yuji Itadori encounters cursed objects and becomes host to Sukuna.

a prequel episode set in the year 2006, focusing on the past of Satoru Gojo and Suguru Geto during their second year as students at Jujutsu High.

Episode 2 — Hidden Inventory, Part 2

Yuji Itadori encounters cursed objects and becomes host to Sukuna.

Gojo and Geto continue their mission to protect Riko Amanai, the Star Plasma Vessel, from the two opposing factions, leading to Riko's eventual decision and a sudden, tragic twist of events.

Episode 3 — Hidden Inventory, Part 3

Yuji Itadori encounters cursed objects and becomes host to Sukuna.

Satoru Gojo, Suguru Geto, and Riko Amanai rescue Riko's caretaker, Kuroi, which is followed by the beginning of the intense confrontation with the assassin Toji Fushiguro and the tragic turning points of the arc.

Episode 4 — Hidden Inventory, Part 4

Yuji Itadori encounters cursed objects and becomes host to Sukuna.

Depicts the final, brutal confrontations with the assassin Toji Fushiguro, culminating in Satoru Gojo's mastery of his powers and a dark turning point for Suguru Geto.

Episode 5 — Premature Death

Yuji Itadori encounters cursed objects and becomes host to Sukuna.

Concludes the "Hidden Inventory/Premature Death" arc by detailing the divergent paths of Satoru Gojo and Suguru Geto one year after Riko Amanai's death, which ultimately leads to Geto embracing an extremist ideology.

Episode 6 — It's Like That

Shifts focus to the current timeline (October 2018), marking the start of the "Shibuya Incident Arc". The episode follows Yuji Itadori, Megumi Fushiguro, and Nobara Kugisaki on a normal day that quickly turns into a mission regarding a potential traitor within Jujutsu High.

Episode 7 — Evening Festival

Concludes the intense battle between Mechamaru and Mahito and then jumps forward in time to Halloween night, when the large-scale "Shibuya Incident" officially begins.

Episode 8 — The Shibuya Incident

A cursed veil descends upon Shibuya on Halloween night, trapping thousands of civilians and forcing Satoru Gojo to confront a formidable alliance of curses and curse users.

Episode 9 — The Shibuya Incident - Gate Open

The multi-pronged operation by the curses begins as Satoru Gojo is lured into the center of Shibuya and confronted by several special grade curses at once.

Episode 10 — Pandemonium

Focuses on the immediate aftermath of Satoru Gojo's sealing, as the remaining sorcerers mobilize to rescue him and the curses regroup to plan their next moves.

Episode 11 — Seance

Steps away from the Shibuya incident and instead provides backstory for a key character from Season 1, setting up a major conflict for Yuji Itadori.

Episode 12 — Dull Knife

The curtain preventing the sorcerers from entering Shibuya Station is finally lifted and the teams can continue their rescue operation. However, with Ino heavily injured, Yuji and Megumi must split up. Nobara encounters a curse user.

Episode 13 — Red Scale

Focuses entirely on the brutal and strategic battle between Yuji Itadori and the Death Painting Womb, Choso, in the underground station, as Yuji desperately tries to prevent his opponent from reaching Gojo.

Episode 14 — Fluctuations

Several concurrent, high-stakes battles as the sorcerers struggle against the overwhelming power of the cursed spirits, leading to the return of a familiar assassin and critical injuries for the sorcerers.

Episode 15 — Fluctuations - Part 2

The introduction of Toji Zen'in into Dagon's domain, leading to the brutal defeat of the curse and the horrifying resurrection of Ryomen Sukuna.

Episode 16 — Thunderclap

Features two intense, high-voltage confrontations: Megumi Fushiguro vs. Toji Zen'in, and the beginning of the overwhelming battle between Jogo and the newly awakened Sukuna.

Episode 17 — Thunderclap - Part 2

Delivers an intense and destructive battle between Ryomen Sukuna (in Yuji's body) and the powerful shikigami, Divine General Mahoraga, summoned by Megumi Fushiguro.

Episode 18 — Right and Wrong

Yuji regains control of his body, only to witness the damage caused by Sukuna's merciless rampage. Meanwhile, Nanami, badly injured and delirious, confronts a group of transfigured humans and reunites with a familiar foe.

Episode 19 — Right and Wrong - Part 2

Yuji and Mahito's fight continues. However, Yuji is unaware that Mahito has already divided into two beings, one of which is battling Nobara elsewhere. He intends to use her to permanently crush Yuji's spirit.

Episode 20 — Right and Wrong - Part 3

Yuji's determination falters when Mahito attacks Nobara, but just as things appear bleak, Yuji's best friend arrives, reigniting his spirit. Now all three go beyond their limits as they fight to kill.

Episode 21 — Metamorphosis

Mahito's fight against Yuji and Todo continues. Thanks to everything that happened during his battle with these sorcerers, Mahito finally discovers the true nature of his soul and takes on a new deadly form.

Episode 22 — Metamorphosis - Part 2

The Shibuya incident is coming to an end and many groups of jujutsu sorcerers are getting ready for what may happen next. Meanwhile, Yuji's victory over Mahito is short-lived as the two are confronted by the mastermind behind everything.

Episode 23 — Shibuya Incident - Gate, Close

The Shibuya Incident comes to a close. Yuki and Geto argue over their ideals of cursed energy. A long awaited sorcerer returns to the cursed filled Japan to execute.
